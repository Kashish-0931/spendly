Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter to track your spending.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as your browser type, device, and log entries generated when you access the Service.

We do not ask for or store bank account numbers, card details, or other payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and secure your account and authenticate you when you sign in.
  • Store, display, and summarise the expenses you record.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the Service.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures — such as password hashing and restricted access — to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may delete your expense records or close your account at any time, after which your data is removed except where we are required to retain it by law.

4. Third Party Services

The Service may rely on third party providers for infrastructure such as hosting, database storage, and web fonts. These providers process data only as needed to perform their services and are expected to keep it confidential and secure.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. If the Service integrates additional third party services in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect that.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to access, correct, or delete your information, please contact us through the support channels listed within the Service.
